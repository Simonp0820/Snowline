Shortlist locally

The directory covers all 2021 Canadian census subdivisions and all 2024 U.S. Census places and counties in the 50 states and D.C. Municipal equivalents and rural areas are included. Neighbourhoods are not separate records. U.S. territories are outside this directory. Counties overlap places and are not comparable to a single neighbourhood.

Interpret snow carefully

A market uses the nearest station with a published annual snowfall normal in the same country, within 50 km of its representative point. Both countries use 1991–2020. Missing station coverage stays unknown. Station quality, mountain elevation, lake effects and large rural boundaries limit the comparison. More snow can increase both demand and costs.

Validate buying demand

Households include renters and apartments. Ownership is not detached housing. Neither identifies retired homeowners or willingness to pay. Do not multiply unrelated population shares to invent a customer count.

Insights summarize the selected evidence. Local competition, detached-property mix, route density, customer acquisition and employer costs remain research questions. Calculations and financial limitations

Automatic price: one listed seasonal package per independent provider covering driveway, walkway and steps. Multiple qualifying providers use an unweighted arithmetic mean, rounded to cents; one provider is labelled separately. Starting prices, unclear service scope and obsolete season offers are excluded. Reviews expire after 180 days. The current sample covers Winnipeg, Fargo, West Fargo, Moorhead (Minnesota) and Calgary; coverage follows explicit provider service areas. Most directory markets have no pricing sample yet. This is a research snapshot, not live price collection. Tax treatment, season dates, property size, response times and extras may differ; inspect the linked sources. No season-length proration is invented.

Missing price: revenue, profit and break-even remain unavailable until a local price is entered. Operating costs and route capacity can still be modelled. A custom price is labelled as your assumption and stays attached to its market.

Revenue = customers × seasonal price.

Labour = customers × visits × elapsed minutes per property ÷ 60 × crew size × cost per worker-hour.

Other variable costs = customers × (visits × supplies/fuel per visit + acquisition cost).

Operating profit before tax = revenue − labour − other variable costs − seasonal fixed operating costs. Include owner time in labour or management costs.

Break-even customers = fixed costs ÷ contribution per customer, rounded up. Non-positive contribution cannot cover fixed costs by adding customers.

Route hours = customers × elapsed minutes per property ÷ 60. One locally based crew is assumed. Include driving in elapsed time; repeat passes and breakdowns need additional allowance.

Excluded from operating profit: tax, financing, startup purchases and working-capital needs. Model these separately before committing to expansion. Costs may increase in steps if another crew is needed.

Currency: model inputs use each market's local currency; results convert using your editable exchange rate. Source income figures are not converted into a purchasing-power comparison.

Storage: left/right selections, independent search filters and scenario inputs are saved in this browser when storage is available. Use Reset prices & inputs to restore the calculator. No customer information is collected or sent.
